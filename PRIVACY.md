# Privacy Policy

**AMU (ARK Mod Updater)** is a local desktop application. It does **not** collect,
transmit, or share any personal data, and it contains **no telemetry or
analytics** of any kind.

## What stays on your computer

- Your configured servers, mods, settings and logs are stored in a local
  **SQLite database** on your machine.
- Steam login credentials (if you choose to enter them) are encrypted at rest
  with the **Windows Data Protection API (DPAPI)**, tied to your Windows user
  account. They never leave your computer in readable form and are never sent
  to the developer.

## Network connections AMU makes (only for its core function)

- **Valve / Steam** — to download Workshop mods via SteamCMD and to read public
  Workshop item pages (mod name, preview image, update date).
- **GitHub** — to check for and download application updates from this
  repository's releases.
- **Your own ARK servers** — RCON commands over your local network / the address
  you configure.

No usage data, identifiers, or personal information is sent to the developer or
any third party. All of the above are standard requests required for the app to
do its job.
